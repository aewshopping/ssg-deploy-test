Here's the list from last month (history books published here in the UK in hardback): only nine but a wide range of topics. A couple I am curious about are:

- __This Little World: A New History of Tudor and Stuart England__, by _Nandini Das_. I really enjoyed (and reviewed) [Nandini Das' last book](https://popularhistorybooks.com/posts/reviews/2023-07-14-review-courting-india/), so on that evidence I expect this one to be highly readable and insightful. It presents a history of 16th and 17th century England through the broad horizons of at least some of its denizens and visitors.

- __To Rule All Under Heaven: A History of Classical China, from Confucius to the First Emperor__, by _Andrew Seth Meyer_. The blurb states that this is the first ever comprehensive history in English of China's warring states period, which must be an exageration, but certainly I have never read one, and would like to!
