I'm a bit late this month, for which I blame AI. More on this later!

The three that I am looking out for are:

- __Nineteen Days in Autumn: A New History Of 1066__, by _Erin Goeres_. Coinciding with the surprisinly popular tapestry exhbition at the British Musuem, this promises to be a blow-by-blow account of the critical days leading up to the conquest of England in 1066: a tragic but compelling story!

- __Out of Chaos: A Global History of the Nation State__, by _Jon Wilson_. Most countries are young. I think we should generally pay more attention to this. Jon Wilson's new book will, I think, help :-) 

- __Civilizations Map by Map: From Ancient Empires to Forgotten Cultures__, by _various authors_. I don't normally include this sort of coffee table book among the new history books released, but the broad range of fascinating civilizations and excellent colouring-in on the enticing maps means I can't this time resist. It's one that I would like to get for my coffee table.
