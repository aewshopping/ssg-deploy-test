The very latest updates from the past, published in September 2026 (in hardback, in the UK). A few that I would like to have read, even if I probably will not get around to reading them: 

- __The University: A History in Stone, Silk, and Blood__, by _William Whyte_. A university is fundamentally a place[^1], and a place is defined by stuff. Stuff like stone buildings, silk gowns and of course students!

- __The Last Invasion of England: 1688 and the Dutch Armada__, by _Arthur der Weduwen_. In which the glorious revolution is actually a glorious invasion. As the blurb puts it: "The sixteen thousand Dutch troops who accompanied William were not on hand for moral support." 😬

- __Empire at the Edge of the World: Endurance and Madness in the High Arctic__, by _Giles Milton_. Another (presumably) rip-roaring adventure from the doyen of popular history writing!
